How Northline handles information submitted through the website, applications, and partnership forms.

01 / PRIVACY

Information we collect.

Northline may receive information you choose to provide through application, contact, and partnership forms, including your name, email address, social handles, company details, and message content.

How we use it.

We use submitted information to review talent applications, respond to inquiries, evaluate partnership opportunities, and operate the Northline website. We do not sell submitted information.

Third-party services.

Some website features may rely on hosting, form, analytics, or content-delivery providers. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services.

Retention and security.

Northline takes reasonable steps to protect information and keeps it only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected or as required by law.

Your choices.

You can contact Northline to ask about information you have submitted or request correction where applicable.
