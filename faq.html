Answered.

A quick guide for talent, brands, and anyone looking to work with Northline.

What does Northline do?

Northline is a talent agency representing creators, competitive players, and digital talent. Representation is the core, with management, partnerships, and supporting services around it.

Who can apply?

Creators, competitive players, and digital talent with a clear direction can apply. We review applications individually.

How does the application work?

Start on the application page, prepare your profile, then copy the application into a Northline Discord ticket for review.

Do you work with brands?

Yes. Brands can use the For Brands page to describe a partnership, explore the current roster, and build a clear brief.

Are talent stats displayed?

Only public information that Northline has verified and intentionally chosen to publish. We do not invent follower counts, earnings, placements, or rankings.

What creative services are available?

Creative is supporting work around the agency's talent focus. Current listed services include video, posters, banners, overlays, jerseys, logos, team banners, full branding, coaching, and social management.

Can I request a specific talent?

Yes. Include the talent name or category in your brand brief. Availability and deal terms are confirmed separately.

Is Northline accepting partnerships?

Brands and partners can start with the brand brief or contact page. A perfect brief is not required to start the conversation.
