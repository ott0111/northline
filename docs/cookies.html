Cookie Policy
Northline.

Information about cookies and similar technologies used on the Northline website.

01 / COOKIES

How cookies are used.

This site stores your cookie preference so it can remember whether you accepted optional cookies. Essential browser storage is used for basic website functionality. The preference cookie is called northline_cookie_consent and expires after six months.

Optional technologies.

Optional analytics cookies are off by default. You can choose whether to allow them in the cookie banner, but no analytics service is currently connected, so accepting optional analytics does not enable third-party tracking at this time.

Your controls.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings control at the bottom-left of the website, or through your browser settings. Essential storage is always active because it remembers your preference.
