Northline / About

Talent first.
By design.

Northline is an independent talent agency built around people, opportunities, and the long game.

01 / THE IDEA

Representation should feel like a team.

Northline exists to give talent a stronger foundation around the career they're already building.

We work across representation, partnerships, management, and supporting creative services. That means fewer disconnected conversations and more people working from the same direction.

We're early by design. Instead of trying to look bigger than we are, we're focused on building a roster, reputation, and network that can last.
